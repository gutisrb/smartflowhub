CRM Infrastructure (The Core)
Build a database (Airtable/PostgreSQL) with the following Statuses and Columns:
• Pipeline Statuses:
    1. Lead Captured (Form submitted)
    2. Qualified (Pre-meeting vetting)
    3. Meeting Booked (Calendly trigger)
    4. Meeting Complete (Post-call status)
    5. Closed (Contract signed)
    6. Lost (Archived)
• Essential Columns:
    ◦ Contact Info (Name, Email, Company, Phone)
    ◦ Youth Labor Needs (Niche, Volume of workers needed)
    ◦ Booking Timestamp (From Calendly)
    ◦ Meeting Date/Time
    ◦ Source (Website Form vs. Chatbot)
    ◦ Nudge Triggered (Boolean: Yes/No)
2. Automated Email Sequence Engine
Set up an n8n workflow to handle these 7 specific emails:
• The Inbound Nudge (The "Leaky Bucket" Fix):
    ◦ Trigger: Website Intake Form submission.
    ◦ Logic: Wait 30 minutes. Check if the email exists in "Meeting Booked" status. If NOT, send Email 1: Complete your booking.
• The Attendance Sequence (The "Show-Up" Logic):
    ◦ Email 2: Immediate Booking Confirmation.
    ◦ Email 3: 24-Hour Reminder before the meeting.
    ◦ Email 4: 1-Hour Reminder.
    ◦ Email 5: 15-Minute Reminder (The "Jumping on now" nudge).
• The Post-Call Logic:
    ◦ Email 6: Recap & Next Steps (Sent if the meeting is marked "Meeting Complete" but not yet "Closed").
    ◦ Email 7: Follow-up/Nurture (Sent 3 days later if still not closed).
3. Front-End Integration (Capture & Enrich)
• Website Intake Form: Connect via Webhook to n8n to create a record in the CRM at Lead Captured status.
• AI Chatbot (Final Layer): An n8n-hosted AI Agent node on the website to:
    ◦ Answer labor regulation/pricing questions.
    ◦ Route qualified businesses to the Meeting Booked status by providing the calendar link directly.
4. Dashboard Tracking (The "Cockpit")
Create a reporting view to track these 5 critical metrics:
1. Total Registrations/Leads (Form/Chatbot entries)
2. Booking Rate (% of leads who booked a call)
3. Show-Up Rate (% of booked calls that were "Meeting Complete")
4. Conversion Rate (% of meetings that moved to "Closed")
5. Average Time to Close (From Lead Captured to Closed)
Future Integration Note
The system must be designed so that Outbound Workflows (e.g., Clay/Instantly) can be connected later as a new source to feed leads directly into this same CRM and sequence engine.
