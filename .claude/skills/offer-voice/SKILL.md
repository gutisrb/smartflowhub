# Offer voice

First read, in full: `/Users/johhn/Library/Mobile Documents/iCloud~md~obsidian/Documents/Smartflow/wiki/offer-voice.md`
Then `wiki/copywriting.md` §1-§5 and `photonic-lunar/offer-brief.md`. Do not write a line before that.

The ten laws, if the file cannot be read:

1. In one glance the viewer must believe four things: **it sells · it runs a real sales process written for my business · I stay in control · this is how selling is done now.** Register: **calm authority, the process is the subject** ("Agent vodi Vaš prodajni proces."). Nikola's blunt "AGENT MAKE VERY GOOD SALES. AGENT MAKE MONEY" is the IDEA, never the tone: "i to vrhunski" was rejected as cringe and too bold.
2. **Claim layer is general, proof layer is specific.** Cards and headlines state the promise, identical across businesses wherever possible. Chats, CRM rows and the catalogue carry the specifics.
3. **Never put the demo's story in a claim:** no named customer, no handled objection, no package or price, no country.
4. **Universality test:** could the line sit unchanged on any lead's demo? If yes, ship it. Say objectively when a universal line is possible.
5. **Simple, direct, short.** Outcome before mechanism; one clause of mechanism at most. Do not beat around the bush.
6. **Confident, not loud.** Off limits in claim text: superlatives (vrhunski, najbolji), the word "novac", claims about their inbox ("poruke koje već dobijate"). Never invent numbers, plans, prices or processes the lead's own site does not state.
7. **Never:** price (call only), cost saving / hours / headcount, "besplatno i bez obaveza", claims about their team or losses, leadovi / automatizacija / bot / chatbot / alat / jeftino, naming the cheap category, Smartflow as a product brand.
8. **Built for them, with Nikola;** the process is written together on the call, never "set up from your website". Hand-over at the edge of competence, data stays theirs, first month on their real messages said once, as confidence.
9. **Would a blank ChatGPT write it?** Then it is not good enough.
10. **Output ONE recommendation, at most two alternatives ranked, one sentence why.** No menus, no long self-reflection. Rejected twice in the same direction, or two of his instructions pull apart (blunt vs professional): stop guessing and ASK with side-by-side previews.
